The green corridor: what a paper about only these two theorems would contain.

The green underlay is the transitive closure of the proof prerequisites of revenueEquivalence and taxationPrinciple, traced backwards. It is computed, not curated — · declarations out of the whole development. Tick storyline only to see it alone. Everything not green is, for this particular paper, optional.

What it contains: the two value functions and their Lipschitz lemmas, IsIC, surplus, the whole IC ⟹ envelope bridge, surplus_split, and then MechHom → transfer_eq_of_iso / mechIso_of_sameAlloc_sameRent → masterTheorem_transferInvariance → both corollaries. That is the unification, end to end.

What it does not contain — and this is the point: T, Q, adj_T_Q, T_initial, Mech₀, equivAllocMech₀, SingleCrossing. The green cone reaches the category (objects, morphisms, isomorphisms in Mech) but never the adjunction. A paper that wanted nothing but the unification could stop at Move 2's isomorphism and never define a functor. IsIR appears in the cone only because ICIRMechanism is defined with it as a field — no proof step on the storyline uses it.

So there are two honest papers here, and the graph tells you which is which. The narrow one: one theorem, two corollaries, and an isomorphism that explains why. The wide one, which is what the repository actually proves: the narrow one plus the adjoint structure that says where the master theorem comes from — T ⊣ Q, the rent-stripping comonad, and Alloc ≌ Mech₀. The wide claim is true and non-trivial; it is simply not load-bearing for the two corollaries, and the green corridor is the proof of that.

What the hatching shows: where the category theory actually bites.

Trace the hatched nodes and you can see the categorical layer's real shape. It is not a spine running through the development — it is a region that the analysis feeds into, with exactly one place where it feeds back out.

It bites here: transfer_eq_of_iso — an isomorphism in Mech forces equal transfers, because antisymmetry applies to both the allocation order and the surplus order. That single lemma is on the red bridge, and it is why the category is load-bearing rather than decorative. Under the old transfer-ordered MechHom this implication was unavailable; under an allocation-only order it was false.

It does not bite here: adj_T_Q, T_full, T_faithful, T_initial, T_preservesColimits, Q_preservesLimits, equivAllocMech₀ — the entire Move 1 / Move 3 apparatus. Nothing downstream consumes any of it. These are proved structure about the framework: true, non-trivial, and consumed by no theorem. That is what an adjunction is, and contriving a dependency on it to make this picture look better would be dishonest.

The honest summary for a paper: the two classical theorems are unified by one master theorem, and the category records why — but they are not derived from the adjoint functor theorem.

1. adj_T_Q has no consumer. Only T_preservesColimits and Q_preservesLimits sit downstream of it; neither corollary reaches it.
