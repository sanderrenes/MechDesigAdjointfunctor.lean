# The Myerson–Mirrlees Master Theorem, in Lean

A Lean 4 / Mathlib formalisation arguing that Myerson's (1981) Revenue Equivalence Theorem and
the Mirrlees (1971) Taxation Principle are both instances of a single categorical theorem: the
transfer functor **T : Alloc → Mech** is left adjoint to the allocation functor **Q : Mech → Alloc**
(`T ⊣ Q`), and `T(q)` is the minimal-rent mechanism for the allocation rule `q`: every IC-IR mechanism
with that rule sits above it, and is isomorphic to it once the lowest type's rent is zero.
